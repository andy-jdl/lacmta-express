Privacy Policy

Last Updated: Mon Sep 14 2026 17:00:00 GMT-0700 (Pacific Daylight Time)

Intro & Scope

This privacy notice describes how your information (phone number, message contents and timestamps) are stored when you use our service. We (the maintainers of this service) use Twilio, a cloud communication platform, that allows you (the user) to send and receive SMS text messages. Twilio records message data from every phone number that interacts with this service for 14 days.

How your data is used

Data shall be used for debugging and development purposes only. Outside of Twilio logging, we do not retain any information or data. We will not sell, replicate or distribute any information we collect within Twilio message logs.
