What this service does

You send a text with a bus line number (e.g. "LACMTA 420") and receive a reply with estimated arrival times. No account, no signup or no ongoing messages beyond your reply.

Message frequency

You receive one reply per message you send. We do not send unsolicited messages.

Accuracy

Arrival times are estimates pulled from LA Metro's public data and may be delayed, inaccurate, or unavailable.
